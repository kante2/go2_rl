How the comparison is made

Both traces are 20 ms RMS levels in dBFS, grouped into 50 ms bins. The shaded gap and chart 3 use Predicted − Measured: positive means the model predicts louder than measured.

Chart 2 is a trailing average over the selected period. Chart 3 subtracts those two averages.

Audio timing is estimated from SSH receipt times, so the gap includes transport delay and clock drift. Treat these numbers as an approximate trend, not a calibrated error metric.
