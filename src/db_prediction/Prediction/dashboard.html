Timing & units

Both traces show 20 ms RMS levels in dBFS. Predictions are placed at their target center (t + 20 ms). Measurements are placed at their estimated audio window center.

Audio timing uses a nominal sample clock estimated from SSH receipt times. Transport delay and clock drift are not independently corrected. This plot is an approximate time comparison; it does not calculate prediction error or calibrated SPL.
